CurvaTrip — AI Travel Concierge for the YallaGo App

Conversational backend powering the chat and voice screens of the YallaGo flight-booking app. 32 agent tools sit over a live GDS: flight search and refinement, fare rules, booking gated behind SATIM payment, price alerts, and day-by-day trip plans with photos, weather and distances. Agent runtime built on the open-source Hermes Agent (MIT); the travel product, language stack and voice bridge are my own code.

Hotel Contract SaaS — LLM Extraction & Pricing Engine

Multi-tenant B2B platform that turns hotel contracts (PDF / DOCX / XLSX) into a normalized Oracle schema of 17 tables through an LLM extraction pipeline with an OCR fallback, then prices any stay with a deterministic pure-Python engine — no LLM anywhere in the pricing path. Hermes, the in-app tool-calling assistant, answers questions, finds missing fields and proposes edits behind a dry-run → confirm flow. Includes row-level tenant isolation from the JWT, an Oracle-backed async upload queue (FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED), parse-quality scoring, audit log and per-event billing.
