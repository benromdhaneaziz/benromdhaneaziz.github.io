Overview

A three-phase study of two economic series from the Federal Reserve Economic Data archive: retail sales for book stores (MRTSSM451211USN) and new privately-owned housing units started in the Northeast (HOUSTNENSA).

Phase one argues a modelling strategy from statistical and graphical analysis — structural breaks, random walk versus polynomial trend, seasonality. Phase two fits the series through differencing and functional forms and compares candidate models. Phase three analyses the residuals to confirm the fit holds.

What it does

  • Stationarity testing with ADF and KPSS
  • Trend and seasonality decomposition, including structural-break detection
  • Variance-stabilising transformations before modelling
  • ARIMA and SARIMA candidates compared on the same series
  • Residual diagnostics as an explicit third phase rather than an afterthought
