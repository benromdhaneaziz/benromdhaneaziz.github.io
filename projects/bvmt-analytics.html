Overview

A multi-page Streamlit application for analysing and predicting stock performance on the Bourse des Valeurs Mobilières de Tunis. Built as a Data Science engineering project at ESPRIT with the Company Value team.

Ten modules cover the market end to end: historical quotations, TUNINDEX and sector indices, dividend forecasting, risk classification, price-direction prediction, clustering, news sentiment, and a RAG chatbot that answers questions about an uploaded PDF.

What it does

  • Price-direction prediction per sector using AdaBoost and Gradient Boosting
  • Stock risk classification into low and high risk bands
  • Dividend forecasting with Prophet
  • Market clustering with DBSCAN over UMAP-reduced features
  • RAG chatbot: upload a PDF and ask questions, backed by LangChain, FAISS and Llama 2 through Ollama
  • Market sentiment from scraped Tunisian financial news
  • Historical data 2016–2023 stored in MongoDB Atlas, collected with Selenium and BeautifulSoup
